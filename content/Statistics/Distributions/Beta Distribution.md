For $\alpha>0, \beta>0$ on $x \in [0,1]$:
$$
f(x) = \frac{x^{\alpha-1}(1-x)^{\beta-1}}{B(\alpha,\beta)}
$$

**Expected Value**
$$
\mathbb{E}[X] = \frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}
$$

**Variance**
$$
\operatorname{Var}(X) = \frac{\alpha\beta}{(\alpha+\beta)^2(\alpha+\beta+1)}
$$
