$$
f(x) =
\begin{cases}
\lambda e^{-\lambda x}, & x \ge 0 \\
0, & x < 0
\end{cases}
$$

**Expected Value**
$$
\mathbb{E}[X] = \frac{1}{\lambda}
$$

**Variance**
$$
\operatorname{Var}(X) = \frac{1}{\lambda^2}
$$

See also: Exponential Likelihood
