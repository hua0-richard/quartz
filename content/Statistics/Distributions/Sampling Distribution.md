A Sampling Distribution is the probability that a distribution of a statistic is obtained through repeated sampling of a specific population.

Suppose $X_i$ is independent and follows $\text{Normal}(\mu, \sigma^2)$ 

$\bar{X} \approx \text{Normal}(\mu, \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{n}})$ 

**Expected Value**
$$
\mathbb{E}[\bar{X}] = \mu
$$

**Variance**
$$
\operatorname{Var}(\bar{X}) = \frac{\sigma^2}{n}
$$
