We say two variables are independent if knowing one provides no information about another.

Mathematically we say,

$$f_{X,Y}​(x,y)=f_X​(x)f_Y​(y)$$
(1) holds  if the variables are independent. Alternatively we may choose to factorize the joint *pdf* into marginal *pdfs*.

$$
f_{Y∣X}​(y∣x)=f_Y​(y) \hspace{1cm} (1)
$$
