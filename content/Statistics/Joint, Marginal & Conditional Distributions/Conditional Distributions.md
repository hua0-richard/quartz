We describe *one* variable *given* *another*.

Conditional *pdf* of $Y$ *given* $X$

$$
f_{Y∣X}​(y∣x)= \frac{fX,Y​(x,y)}{fX​(x)}​
$$

The conditional *pdf* is the joint *pdf* over the marginal *pdf*

>The variable we are conditional on is treated as a constant. In this case it is $x$.

The conditional *pdf* must integrate to 1

$$
∫f_{Y∣X}​(y∣x)dy=1
$$
